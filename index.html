About Vinny

Vinny was born in February 2019 in Egypt, where she presumably oversaw the pyramids before deciding her talents were better spent elsewhere. After a stint as a rescue, she generously agreed to be adopted in July 2023, and has been running the household ever since.

Her résumé includes: world-class cuddling, expert-level purring, and a deeply unprofessional alarm clock service that activates at dawn by sprinting full-speed across her sleeping humans. Tips are not accepted. Breakfast is mandatory.

Recently, Vinny underwent surgery and handled it with the dignity of a cat who knows she'll be getting extra treats out of the deal. She is, by all accounts, a resilient little legend — small in size, enormous in personality, and convinced (correctly) that the world revolves around her.
